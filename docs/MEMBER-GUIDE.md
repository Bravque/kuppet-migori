# KUPPET Migori — Member User Guide

Welcome to the KUPPET Migori member portal. This guide walks you through everything
you can do as a member: joining, logging in, keeping your profile up to date, filing
a Benevolent Fund (BBF) claim, applying for scholarships, and staying informed.

**Portal address:** https://kuppetmigori.co.ke
**Member login:** https://kuppetmigori.co.ke/member/login.html

---

## 1. Getting started

There are two ways your account comes into being.

### A. You register yourself
If you are not yet in the system, use the **Join / Register** link (or go to
https://kuppetmigori.co.ke/member/register.html). Registration is a **3-step form**:

1. **Personal Info** — full name, national ID number, phone, email, gender, date of birth.
2. **Employment** — TSC number, school, sub-county, school category (senior/junior),
   and job group (TSC grade).
3. **Documents** — upload the supporting documents requested on screen.

After you submit, your application is **pending approval**. A branch officer reviews it,
and you will be notified once your membership is **approved** (or if more information is
needed). You cannot use the member features until your account is approved.

### B. Your account was created for you (bulk import)
If the branch registered you from an official roster, your account already exists and is
**active**. Your **temporary password is your national ID number**. The first time you log
in you will be guided through a short setup — see *First login* below.

---

## 2. Logging in

1. Go to https://kuppetmigori.co.ke/member/login.html
2. Enter your **TSC number** (this is your username — not your email) and your **password**.
3. Click **Login**.

> **Note:** After several failed attempts your account is temporarily locked for your
> protection. Wait a few minutes and try again, or reset your password.

### Forgot your password?
1. On the login page click **Forgot password**.
2. Enter the **email address** on your account.
3. Check your inbox for a reset link and follow it to set a new password.

---

## 3. First login (setup wizard)

If your account was imported, the portal walks you through two quick steps before you can
use it. You cannot skip these.

**Step 1 — Set your password.**
Your ID number was only a temporary password. Choose a new one:
- at least **8 characters**,
- includes **a letter and a number**,
- **cannot** be your ID number.

**Step 2 — Complete your profile.**
Fill in every required field so your records are accurate:
phone, email, gender, date of birth, school, sub-county, school category, and job group.
Once all required fields are saved, the portal unlocks and you land on your dashboard.

---

## 4. The dashboard

After login you arrive at your **Dashboard** — a summary of your account:
- your membership status,
- recent BBF claims and scholarship applications with their current status,
- recent notifications,
- quick links to start a new claim or browse scholarships.

The left sidebar is your main menu (on a phone, tap the ☰ menu button to open it):

| Menu item | What it's for |
|-----------|---------------|
| **Dashboard** | Your account summary |
| **My Profile** | View and edit your personal & employment details |
| **BBF Claims** | File and track Benevolent Fund claims |
| **Scholarships** | Browse scholarships you can apply for |
| **My Applications** | Track scholarship applications you've submitted |
| **Notifications** | Messages and status-change alerts |
| **Activity History** | A log of your account activity |

---

## 5. My Profile

Keep your profile current — it feeds directly into your claims and applications.

- Editable details include phone, email, gender, date of birth, school, sub-county,
  school category, and job group.
- Email must be **unique** to your account.
- **Set your school category (senior/junior school) before filing a BBF claim** — the
  claim form needs it and will otherwise block you.

---

## 6. BBF Claims (Benevolent Fund)

The Benevolent Fund supports members in two situations. There are two claim types:

- **Death** — for the death of a relative. You enter the deceased relative's name,
  your relationship to them, and the date of death.
- **Retirement** — for your own retirement. The claim automatically uses your own name;
  you don't re-enter relationship or date of death.

### Filing a claim
1. Go to **BBF Claims → New Claim**.
2. Your own details (TSC number, sub-county, school, school category) are filled in
   automatically from your profile and shown in a read-only box. If your school category
   is missing, set it in **My Profile** first.
3. Choose the claim type and, for a death claim, enter the deceased's details.
4. Upload the required supporting documents:
   - **TSC Slip** (required)
   - **Burial Permit** (required)
   - **Letter from the Principal** (required)
   - **Birth Notification** (optional)
5. Submit.

### Tracking a claim
Open any claim to see its detail page and full timeline. A claim moves through these stages:

`draft → submitted → under review → approved / rejected → paid`

You'll get a **notification** each time the status changes. Amounts (requested/approved)
are set by the reviewing officers.

## 7. Scholarships

The branch offers scholarships in three categories:
- **KCSE** (secondary school leavers),
- **KJSEA** (junior school),
- **DTE** — Diploma in Technical Education.

### Applying
1. Go to **Scholarships** to see what's currently open.
2. Open a scholarship and click **Apply**.
3. Upload the required documents:
   - **Letter of Application** (required)
   - **TSC Slip** (required)
   - plus any others listed (e.g. KCSE certificate, admission letter, fee structure).
4. Submit.

> You can submit **one application per scholarship**. If you try to apply twice for the
> same scholarship the portal will tell you an application already exists.

### Tracking
Go to **My Applications** to follow each one:

`applied → under review → approved / rejected`

Status changes trigger a notification.

---

## 8. Notifications & Activity History

- **Notifications** collects every alert — claim and application status changes, and
  branch messages. The bell icon in the top bar takes you there.
- **Activity History** is a running log of actions on your account, useful for keeping
  track of what you've done and when.

---

## 9. Security tips

- Use a strong, private password and never share it.
- The portal signs you in with a token that lasts up to 30 days; still, always **sign out**
  on shared or public computers.
- If you're ever locked out, use **Forgot password** or contact the branch office.
