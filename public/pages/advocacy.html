Teachers' Rights & Legal Support

KUPPET Migori provides comprehensive advocacy support across all areas of teacher rights and labour relations.

Constitutional & Legal Rights

Every teacher is protected under Kenya's Constitution and labour laws. Article 41 guarantees fair labour practices, the right to join unions, and the right to strike. Understanding these protections is your first line of defence.

Learn Your Rights

Collective Bargaining Agreement

The CBA negotiated between KUPPET and TSC governs your salary, allowances, leave entitlements, and working conditions. Know what you're entitled to under the current agreement and how to claim it.

View CBA Documents

Disciplinary Proceedings

If you are facing disciplinary action, you have the right to representation, fair hearing, and appeal. Contact KUPPET Migori immediately — do not attend any disciplinary hearing without union representation.

Get Representation

Workplace Harassment & Discrimination

Harassment and discrimination are illegal and unacceptable. Whether it's sexual harassment, gender-based discrimination, ethnic bias, or any other form of mistreatment, KUPPET Migori will stand with you.

Report Harassment

Wrongful Transfer & Deployment

Transfers must follow TSC guidelines. If you believe you've been transferred unfairly, punitively, or without following proper procedures, KUPPET Migori's advocacy team will review your case and take action.

Challenge a Transfer

Labour Court Representation

For cases requiring legal proceedings at the Employment and Labour Relations Court (ELRC), KUPPET Migori provides legal support and representation to members, ensuring every teacher has access to justice.

Quick Reference

Key Legal Protections for Teachers

Employment Act 2007

  • Written employment contract entitlement
  • Minimum 21 days annual leave
  • 3 months maternity / 2 weeks paternity leave
  • Protection from unfair dismissal
  • Right to severance pay on redundancy

Labour Relations Act 2007

  • Right to join and form trade unions
  • Right to collective bargaining
  • Protection from anti-union discrimination
  • Right to protected industrial action
  • Dispute resolution through mediation

TSC Act 2012

  • Right to fair hearing in disciplinary matters
  • Right to union representation
  • Right to appeal decisions
  • Career progression entitlements
  • Transfer rights and hardship provisions
