About KUPPET Migori

Three decades of unwavering commitment to the rights, welfare, and professional excellence of post-primary education teachers in Migori County.

Who We Are

Our Purpose & Principles

Our Vision

A respected, professional, and empowered teaching force that drives educational excellence and national development in Migori County and Kenya at large.

Our Mission

To promote and protect the rights, welfare, and professional interests of post-primary education teachers in Migori County through collective bargaining, advocacy, and professional development.

Our Commitment

We are committed to ensuring every teacher in Migori County works with dignity, receives fair compensation, and has access to professional growth opportunities and welfare support.

Guiding Principles

Our Core Values

Integrity

We uphold the highest standards of honesty, transparency, and accountability in all our dealings with members and stakeholders.

Unity

We believe in the strength of collective action. Together, KUPPET members speak with one powerful voice that commands attention and respect.

Justice

We advocate for fair treatment, equal opportunity, and equitable working conditions for every teacher, regardless of gender, region, or background.

Professionalism

We champion continuous professional development and hold our members to the highest standards of educational excellence and ethical conduct.

Service

We are driven by a genuine commitment to serve our members — every program, campaign, and initiative is designed around the teacher's needs.

Accountability

Our leaders are accountable to members. We maintain transparent financial management and regular reporting on all union activities and resources.

Inclusivity

We celebrate diversity and ensure every member — regardless of gender, age, or sub-county — has equal voice and representation in KUPPET Migori.

Advocacy

We are unapologetic in our advocacy for better pay, safer working environments, and policy reforms that elevate the teaching profession.

Our Journey

Branch History

From humble beginnings to becoming the strongest teachers' voice in Migori County.

KUPPET Migori Branch was established in 1994, shortly after the formation of KUPPET National. The branch was set up to provide organised representation for post-primary education teachers in the newly formed Migori District (now County), which had previously been served as part of the South Nyanza region.

In its early years, the branch focused primarily on establishing sub-branches across the district's diverse sub-counties, recruiting members, and building awareness of teachers' rights under the employment law framework of the time.

Over three decades, KUPPET Migori has grown from a small regional office into a powerful union representing over 4,500 teachers across all seven sub-counties of Migori County, covering more than 320 secondary and technical institutions.

1994

Branch Founded

KUPPET Migori Branch formally established following the promulgation of KUPPET National. First branch offices opened in Migori Town.

1998

Sub-Branch Network Established

Sub-branches established across all sub-counties including Uriri, Suna East, Suna West, Nyatike, Kuria East, and Kuria West.

2003

First CBA Victory

KUPPET Migori played a pivotal role in the first Collective Bargaining Agreement that delivered a major salary increment for teachers.

2010

BBF Program Expanded

The Benevolent and Burial Fund was expanded to cover immediate family members, providing greater security to all members.

2013

Migori County Devolution

With Kenya's devolution, KUPPET Migori restructured to align with the new county governance framework, strengthening local advocacy capacity.

2017

Teacher Resource Centre Launched

Launched the Teachers Resource Centre providing professional development materials, legal resources, and curriculum support to all members.

2024

30 Years of Service

Celebrated 30 years of service to Migori teachers, now representing over 4,500 members. Launched digital services for members.
