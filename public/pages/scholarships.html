Scholarship Application Tips

1 Check Eligibility First

Read all eligibility criteria carefully before investing time in an application. Confirm your KUPPET membership status is current.

2 Prepare Documents Early

Gather all required documents well before the deadline: KCSE certificates, admission letters, national ID, KUPPET membership proof, and bank details.

3 Write a Strong Essay

Many scholarships require a personal statement. Visit the KUPPET branch office for guidance on writing compelling scholarship essays.

Need help with your application? Visit the KUPPET Migori Branch office or call us during office hours. Our staff will assist you with the application process.
