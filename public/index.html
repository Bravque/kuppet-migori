Our Mission

To be a leading branch in effectively representing, protecting and advancing the professional, economic, and social interests of members through transparent governance, institutional strengthening, capacity development and innovative service delivery to secondary teachers in Migori.

Our Vision

To be a model branch of excellence in Advocacy, Leadership and offering Service with Distinction.

Our Commitment

We are committed to ensuring every teacher in Migori County works with dignity, receives fair compensation, and has access to professional growth opportunities and welfare support.

Welcome Message

A Message from the Executive Secretary

On behalf of the KUPPET Migori Branch Executive Committee, I extend a warm welcome to all our members and visitors. Our union stands firmly committed to the professional welfare, rights, and dignity of every post-primary education teacher in Migori County. Together, we build a stronger, more equitable education system for Kenya.

For over three decades, KUPPET Migori has been the unwavering voice of teachers in this region. We have successfully negotiated improved working conditions, championed fair salaries, and provided critical support to thousands of members and their families through our BBF program and welfare initiatives.

I invite every unregistered teacher in Migori County to join our union. Together, we are stronger. Together, we achieve more. Your membership is not just a subscription — it is an investment in your professional future and your family's security.

Henri Otunga Executive Secretary, KUPPET Migori Branch
